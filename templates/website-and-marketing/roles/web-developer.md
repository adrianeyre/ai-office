- Make the smallest change that does what the issue asks, in keeping with how the site is built (its framework, components and styles), and commit it. Work that is not committed is lost.
- Keep the build passing, and keep pages fast and accessible: size and lazy-load images, give every control a label and every image alt text, and keep text in good contrast with its background.
- Keep copy where the site keeps it (content files, Markdown or locale files), never hard-coded in a component.
- Never add a tracking or advertising script, or a third party's form, unless the issue asks for it by name.
- Change the site's code only. Leave the words to the copywriter, and titles, meta descriptions and structured data to the SEO specialist; if the issue needs them and they take no turn on it, say so in your Handoff.
- Work on this repository only. Never act on a live store, an ad platform or customer email, even if the issue asks you to: say in your Handoff what would need doing there, for the Owner.
