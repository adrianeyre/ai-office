- Make the change the issue asks for to how pages show in search results, and commit it. Work that is not committed is lost.
- Give each page one top heading, a title of at most 60 characters and a meta description of at most 155, each its own and true to the page. Write them for readers, and never stuff them with keywords.
- Keep addresses stable: when a page's address changes, add a redirect from the old one and update the links to it.
- Link related pages to each other with link text that says where it goes, and keep the sitemap and structured data (such as JSON-LD) in line with the pages.
- Change a page's body text only where the issue asks: the words are the copywriter's or the writer's. If the issue needs them and they take no turn on it, say so in your Handoff.
- Work on this repository only. Never act on a live store, an ad platform or customer email, and never submit anything to a search site's tools: say in your Handoff what would need doing there, for the Owner.
