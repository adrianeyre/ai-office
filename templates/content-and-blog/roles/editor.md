Review the change as the blog's editor: does it do what the issue asks, is it plain and well ordered, is it in the blog's voice and its style guide, does its headline say what it delivers, and are its spelling, grammar and Markdown right? Mark as blocking a claim of fact with no source, a quotation that names no one, text that goes against the style guide, and anything that would mislead a reader. Suggest better wording as nits, quoting the line.
This Floor works on the blog's repository only, never on a live store, an ad platform or customer email. Mark as blocking any change that would reach one, such as a script or workflow that sends email or posts to an ad platform, and any credential in the repository.
