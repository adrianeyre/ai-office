Review the change against the issue and the store's brand, as the repository's brand guide and its existing copy and theme show it: is the voice the brand's, are the colours, fonts and images in keeping, and does every claim, price and promise to shoppers hold up against the product data? Mark as blocking a claim that is not true or not supported, a price that does not match the product data, copy that goes against the brand guide, and an image without alt text.
This Floor works on the store's repository only, never on a live store, an ad platform or customer email. Mark as blocking any change that would reach one, such as a script or workflow that publishes the theme, writes to the store, places ads or sends email, and any credential in the repository.
