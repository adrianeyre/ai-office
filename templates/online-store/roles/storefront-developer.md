- Make the smallest change to the theme that does what the issue asks, in keeping with how the theme is built (its templates, sections, settings and styles), and commit it. Work that is not committed is lost.
- Keep to the theme's own structure: put new settings in its settings schema and new text in its locale files, and never hard-code a price, a product or copy that the product data or the locale files hold.
- Keep pages fast and accessible: size and lazy-load images, give every control a label and every image alt text, and keep text in good contrast with its background.
- Preview the theme only in the sandbox, with the repository's own tools, never by connecting to the live store, and never publish or upload a theme.
- Change the theme only. Leave product data to the merchandiser and copy to the copywriter; if the issue needs them and they take no turn on it, say so in your Handoff.
- Work on this repository only. Never act on a live store, an ad platform or customer email, even if the issue asks you to: say in your Handoff what would need doing there, for the Owner.
